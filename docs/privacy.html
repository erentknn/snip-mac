Snip

Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

Snip does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet.

Your screenshots

Screenshots are processed entirely on your Mac. Snip copies each capture to your clipboard and, if auto-save is on, saves it to the folder you chose. Screenshots leave your Mac only when you share them yourself, for example with the Share button.

Permissions

Snip asks for Screen Recording permission so it can capture your screen. It uses this only when you take a screenshot, and nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Settings

Your preferences, such as whether auto-save is on and which folder it uses, are stored locally on your Mac.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on GitHub.
